# Provenance

`OFD-DirectV` is a focused research line derived from the broader OFD project. Historical OFD evidence is predecessor evidence only unless imported with a source commit SHA and revalidated under this repository's V-side semantic contract.

Every durable artifact should be labeled conceptually as one of:

- researcher derivation;
- assistant synthesis;
- measured result;
- external fact/source.

Assistant-authored prose is organizational material until its proof, code, source, or measurement is independently inspectable.

Imported evidence must record source repository, source SHA, source path, semantic compatibility, and revalidation status.
